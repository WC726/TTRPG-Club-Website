Action Economy 101

In one combat round, every character gets one action, one bonus action, one reaction, and movement.


Actions

Actions cover almost all of the things you can do on a turn. The most basic thing, a weapon attack, takes one action, unless you have an ability that states otherwise.
Casting a spell or cantrip with a casting time of one action also takes one action. The majority of spells and cantrips take one action to cast.
Drinking a potion or using any tool or kit also takes one action.
You can also take the Dash, Dodge, Disengage, or Hide action. The Dash action spends your action to double the distance you can move on this turn. The Dodge action spends your action to give all attacks against you, until the start of your next turn, disadvantage, and also gives you advantage on Dex saves if you can see your attacker. The Disengage action spends your action to allow you to move out of the melee range of an opponent without being subject to an opportunity attack. The Hide action spends your action to make a stealth check against the passive perception of any creatures that can see you. On a success, you gain the benefits of being an unseen attacker.
If there is confusion about whether or not an action a player takes should cost one action, refer to the DM's judgement. If you want to do something specific outside of your turn, based on the actions of others, you can "ready your action," in which you specify what action you are going to take and what the trigger(s) will be. This spends your action.


Bonus Actions

Bonus actions are actions that can only be spent on specific things. There are many abilites that take one bonus action to use. If an ability allows you to attack as a bonus action, you can use that ability to make one attack, and still have an action.
Spells and cantrips with a casting time of one bonus action take one bonus action. However, if you cast a spell or cantrip using your bonus action, you cannot cast a spell using your action, you can only cast a cantrip.
Some classes give you the ability to make the Dash, Disengage, or Hide actions using your bonus action. You CANNOT use your action to use any abilities that cost a bonus action, nor can you cast any spells with a casting time of one bonus action using your action.


Reactions

Reactions are specific abilities that happen outside of your turn, in response to the actions of another entity. An opportunity attack is a reaction that is triggered by a hostile creature moving out of melee range from you. It allows you to immediately make an attack on them, before they move out of range.
